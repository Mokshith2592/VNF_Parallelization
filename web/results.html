What is and is not comparable
  • Same setting: 60 nodes, SFC length 7, 30 requests, parallelism 0.5, link delay U[10,15] ms, link bandwidth U[100,300] Mbps, node capacity U[100,300], VNF demand U[20,50], processing U[0.045,0.2] ms per packet, data size U[50,100] packets, request bandwidth U[30,60] Mbps.
  • Different: random topologies (Waxman, not the paper's exact GT-ITM graphs); instance capacity 100 with per-request load U[10,30] (the paper does not give them); replication and integration cost 0.02 ms per packet per extra branch (the paper does not give τ); LOA search capped at k = 256 and 200 candidates per anchor; 10 runs per point, as in the paper.
  • Not reproduced: the DNF / ParaDSP / PODPR baselines, the heat-map (Fig. 14) and the scalability index (Fig. 16).
  • Serial* and Parallel* are our stand-ins (fully serial or fully parallel layers, placed with VDA), not the exact algorithms of reference [17].
  • Absolute delays depend on the random topologies and τ, so compare the shape of the curves and the relative LOA-vs-VDA gap, not the raw milliseconds.
