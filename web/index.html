    How the hybrid works
    
    
      
  • 1. Model (model.cpp): Floyd-Warshall shortest delays, dependency levels,
        every I-SFG of each level (series-parallel: e.g. (f2 → f3) ∥ f4, paper 2 Fig. 1), and the
        number of DPG paths. Latency = longest branch of link delay + μ·lu +
        (out − 1)·τrep·lu (dividing) + (in − 1)·τint·lu (merging).
    • 
      
  • 2. Seed (sca_vda.cpp): SCA picks the I-SFG with the lowest
        placement-free delay; VDA places VNFs greedily near their predecessors.
    • 
      
  • 3. LOA / ROA (loa.cpp, paper 1): k cheapest DPG paths → feasibility →
        stability filter → coverage → anchors → prune when the next path is costlier than the best.
        The anchor's VNFs keep their hosts, so only part of the space is looked at.
    • 
      
  • 4. Full search (full.cpp): every I-SFG (in order of its placement-free
        delay) × every placement, branch and bound, starting from the best of steps 2 and 3.
    • 
      
  • 5. Certify (hybrid.cpp):
        Optimal when the full search finished;
        Optimal (proven) when it hit its time budget but LOA stopped on its own bound (latency only);
        Bounded otherwise: the best scheme found, with paper 2's Theorem 1 bound.
